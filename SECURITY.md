# Security and privacy

Report suspected credential exposure, unsafe file handling, or privacy bypasses privately to the repository owner rather than opening a public issue containing sensitive data.

Do not attach real credentials, private keys, resumes, customer records, internal URLs, unpublished research material, or generated documents containing personal information. Provide a minimal synthetic reproduction.

The built-in scanner is a defense-in-depth check, not a guarantee of anonymity or secret detection. Users remain responsible for reviewing source material and final documents before sharing them.
